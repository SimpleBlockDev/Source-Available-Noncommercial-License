# Source-Available Noncommercial License (SANL)

*English · [Русский](README.ru.md)*

This repository contains the **Source-Available Noncommercial License (SANL), Version 2.0** and the templates needed to apply it to a software project. SANL is published here as a plain text file, in its official and unchanged form.

## 1. Purpose

SANL exists to keep a project free for the community and to prevent that free work from being turned into paid products and subscriptions. It makes free use of the code available to everyone, and it reserves commercial use to the project's author, who decides on it individually.

## 2. Principle

1. **The community uses the work free of charge.** Personal, educational, research, and other non-commercial use requires no payment and no individual permission.
2. **Paid products and subscriptions are excluded.** The code may not be used in a product or service that charges for access, sells goods or privileges, runs subscriptions or microtransactions, carries advertising, or is otherwise operated for income, unless the author has granted a separate license.
3. **The author of the project grants the license.** In each project, the Licensor is the author or copyright holder of that project, not the author of this repository. The Licensor alone decides whom to license commercially, may keep lists of excluded and licensed parties, and may require anyone to stop using the code if the work they publish has a commercial part. In case of such use the license terminates automatically.
4. **The author is credited.** Whoever builds on the work states where it comes from.
5. **The text is not changed.** SANL is used as published. A changed text is not SANL and must not carry its name (see Section 4).

## 3. Applying SANL to a project

1. Download the unchanged text of the chosen version and save it as LICENSE.md in the root of your project: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SimpleBlockDev/Source-Available-Noncommercial-License/2.0/LICENSE.md`. Use the address with the version tag, so that the text you receive is exactly that version.
2. Replace only the placeholders: `[PROJECT]`, `[AUTHOR]`, `[YEAR]`, `[REPO_URL]`, `[CONTACT]`.
3. Create your own EXCLUDED.md and LICENSED.md next to it, starting from the empty templates in this repository. Each author keeps these lists for their own project alone: this repository keeps no list and no entry here applies to any project. EXCLUDED.md lists the parties to whom you grant no license. LICENSED.md lists the parties to whom you give permission in advance, with its scope and term.
4. Put the notice from the Appendix of SANL at the top of every source file.
5. State in your README that the project is distributed under SANL, Version 2.0, and give the required credit text.

If you want to give users more than SANL gives, do it through an entry in LICENSED.md or a separate written license, not by editing the text.

## 4. Integrity and name of the text

The text of SANL must not be changed. Filling in the placeholders, making entries in EXCLUDED.md and LICENSED.md, placing the Appendix notice in source files, and publishing a marked unofficial translation are not changes. Every other change makes the text a different license. A changed text must not be called "Source-Available Noncommercial License", "SANL", or by the identifier `LicenseRef-SANL-2.0`, or by any confusingly similar name, and must not claim to be SANL. The rule is in Section 10.7 of SANL, and the terms of use of the text are in [TEXT-LICENSE.md](TEXT-LICENSE.md). The unchanged text may be used by anyone, free of charge and without permission.
